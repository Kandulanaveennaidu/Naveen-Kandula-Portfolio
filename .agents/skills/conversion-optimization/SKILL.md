# Conversion Optimization Architecture

## 1. Five Questions Every Section Must Answer
1. Who are you?
2. What can you build?
3. Have you actually built things? (Proof & Case Studies)
4. Why should I trust you? (Product thinking, full-stack ownership)
5. How can I work with you & contact you?

## 2. Frictionless Contact UX
- Low-friction multi-attribute inquiry form (Name, Email, Company, Project Type, Budget, Timeline, Scope).
- One-click copy email button with instant toast confirmation.
- Clear statement of remote availability and response turnaround time.
- Direct links placeholder to verified channels (GitHub, LinkedIn, WhatsApp).
